PART A — FINDINGS (current contract surface)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1) FIVE PARALLEL NAME-MAPPING ARTEFACTS
   Three tables reconcile the same semantic quantities:
     - _SCANNER_TO_FEATURE        src/neutralgrid/backtest/candidate_pipeline.py:35
     - _SCAN_TO_FEATURE           src/neutralgrid/training/unified_training_builder.py:76
     - _INFERENCE_FEATURE_ALIASES src/neutralgrid/models/meta_labeler.py:144
   Plus two more places that must stay in sync:
     - FeatureSnapshot / to_dict  src/neutralgrid/training/data_generator.py:119
     - ACTIVE_SNAPSHOT_META_FEATURES + META_FEATURE_PROFILES
                                  src/neutralgrid/models/meta_labeler.py:134
   safety-invariants.md §"Feature Pipeline Update Rule" calls out three files;
   the alias table + active-profile constant make it five.

2) utility_score COLLAPSES THREE SEMANTICS
   Writers:
     - scan.py:225               compute_governed_provisional_utility (provisional geometry)
     - regime_validator.py:377   same call, published as regime_utility
     - unified_training_builder._derive_utility_score (L689)   same call
   _SCANNER_TO_FEATURE maps utility_score, scan_utility_score, AND regime_utility
   into a single training column utility_score. The provisional-geometry
   assumption is invisible from the name; agreement relies on all three sites
   calling the same helper.

3) ev_score HAS DISAGREEING WRITERS
   - scan.py:709                     percent-scaled (but ev_score is None at scan)
   - enrich_grid_params.py:1116,1170 compute_score(...).rank_score
                                     = ev_aligned * (1 - total_penalty)
                                     (written to edge_medium_ev_score/edge_big_ev_score)
   - _apply_afml_post_scoring        fills final ev_score after enrich
   - unified_training_builder._derive_ev_score (L700)   result.ev_24h (RAW ev)
   Production rows carry a penalized rank score; training-backfilled rows
   carry a raw ev. Same column, different units, silently mixed.

4) ACTIVE PROFILE vs EMITTED COLUMNS DRIFT
   ACTIVE_SNAPSHOT_META_FEATURES = SNAPSHOT_META_FEATURES_V20260421_BOOTSTRAP
   (meta_labeler.py:134) = 8 features:
     range_prob, survival_prob, utility_score, ou_halflife,
     profit_per_grid_pct, num_grids, grid_spacing_pct, adx_1h
   TRAINING_OUTPUT_COLUMNS: ~50
   EXTRA_META_FEATURES:     ~20
   FeatureSnapshot.to_dict: ~55
   _INFERENCE_FEATURE_ALIASES still lists aliases for 25+ features not in
   the active profile.
   Asymmetries:
     - utility_score in, but trend_prob (its driver) out
     - ev_score excluded from bootstrap
     - survival_prob in, but its estimator source not stamped

5) LEAKAGE GUARD IS SINGLE-SITED
   _KNOWN_LABEL_COLUMNS enforced only at meta_labeler._prepare_features (L529)
   and .train (L716). unified_training_builder backfills features in-place
   (out.at[idx, "utility_score"], out.at[idx, "ev_score"] L983, L990) using
   row dicts that also hold labels. Safety relies on derive helpers reading
   a whitelist; there is no structural barrier.

6) survival_prob USED UNDER THREE GENERATIVE MODELS
   - PnLRanker.compute_ev: fill-Bernoulli AND (1-p)*|sl_pct| boundary loss
   - UtilityScorer.compute_utility: falls back to range_prob when None (L350),
     also drives expected_trend_breakout_loss
   - Gate 4 (micro_osc): MC containment threshold
   Writers (StochasticRegimeChecker vs micro_osc MC) are not stamped on the
   snapshot, so meta sees one scalar with context-dependent meaning.

7) leverage BREAKS TRAIN/SERVE EV BASIS
   PnLRanker.compute_score(...).funding_cost_pct uses leverage
   (pnl_ranker.py:162, 271). Enrich passes g.leverage. But:
     - leverage is NOT in FeatureSnapshot / _SCAN_TO_FEATURE
     - unified_training_builder._derive_ev_score calls compute_score()
       without leverage -> defaults to 10
   Any non-10x candidate gets train != serve EV by a funding term
   proportional to (leverage - 10) * |funding| * H/8.

8) HORIZON NOT CROSS-VALIDATED END-TO-END
   BOT_HORIZON_HOURS is canonical, but four configs carry horizon_hours:
     LabelConfig, BarrierConfig, RankingConfig, UtilityConfig.
   safety-invariants cross-validates hurdle_pct but not horizon. Any drift
   silently desynchronizes enrich EV vs training EV vs label window.

9) MI SCORER STARVES AT SCAN TIME; ENRICH NEVER RE-COMBINES
   scan.py:699-724 MI-combines {similarity_score, profile_proba, ev_score,
   meta_prob}. ev_score is None at scan time. Final ev_score is filled post-
   enrichment by _apply_afml_post_scoring, but scan_score is not recomputed,
   so the frozen composite was always produced from a degraded input set.
